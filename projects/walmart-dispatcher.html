02 / PRODUCT DESIGN

Walmart’s Dispatcher – Multiple trip feature

A clearer status for grocery delivery trips with many deliveries, so store associates can act on them in real time.

Client
Walmart (through ArcTouch)
Role
Senior Product Designer
Skills
  • Interaction design
  • Iconography
  • User testing
  • Visual design

Overview

Walmart built Dispatcher, its own platform to see and take action on its grocery deliveries. The main dashboard is where store associates act on trips in real time. My task was the status of trips with more than two deliveries.

The problem

The dashboard needed to give a clear status for trips with more than two deliveries. The goal was for it to keep working with up to ten individual deliveries in a single trip, without the row becoming hard to read.

What I did

I explored ways to show the progress of every delivery inside one trip row. The exploration above uses a row of small icons, one per delivery, that fill in as the trip moves from pickup to drop-off. A line of text says what is happening and when, for example “Trip in progress - Dropoff ETA 4:42 AM” or “At Pick up - 15 min Delay”, and orange marks what needs attention.

I tested the explorations with users, then refined the visual design, the behavior of the status and the iconography details.

The outcome

Associates see one row per trip that shows where each delivery stands, without opening the trip. Dispatcher was one of several products I designed for Walmart’s supply chain teams. The same work included the Exception Dashboard (delayed and late deliveries across US regions), Dispatcher Lite (a mobile app so associates can solve problems while the driver is still at the store) and the Load & Deliver app for truck drivers.
