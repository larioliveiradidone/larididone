03 / PRODUCT DESIGN

Quizlet – SEO Subject pages

Redesigning the Subject pages that students reach from Google, so they find what they need to study. Traffic grew by 12%.

Client
Quizlet (through ArcTouch)
Role
Senior Product Designer
Skills
  • UX research
  • Data analysis
  • UI design
  • SEO

Overview

One of the teams I worked with at Quizlet was SEO. Their main goal was to rank better for specific keyword searches on Google, so students could find the content they needed to study easily. One of the features we worked on was Subject pages. The existing version was simple and built to create link equity for the whole product.

The problem

I started by analyzing the data of the current pages, level by level:

  • Level 1: 60% of SEO sessions that landed on a Level 1 Subject page ended with a click on another Level 1 category, and 40% with a click on a Level 2 or 3 subcategory.
  • Level 2: after clicking a category, 66.7% of users searched and 33.3% left the site. After clicking search, 20% selected a profile, 20% were prompted to sign up and 20% bounced.
  • Level 3: after clicking a subcategory, 50% of users left Quizlet and 50% selected a study set. Every user who selected a study set then left Quizlet.

What I did

I used the analysis to propose a new UI for the page and compared it with the previous version. The new page organizes the content by type, with tabs for all results, flashcards, textbooks and questions, and a Recommended section at the top. I then detailed the UI design for the team.

The outcome

The redesigned Subject pages increased traffic by 12%. During the same period Quizlet went through a major visual rebrand that affected both marketing and product. I also developed some of the public materials for the launch: App Store and Play Store content, in-app onboarding and 404 pages.
